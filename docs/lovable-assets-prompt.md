# Lovable — Super Trasa assets

Wklej całość do Lovable. Eksport PNG (true alpha, nearest, bez blur). Gra dla dzieci 5–6 lat.

---

Brick-built toy game art (NOT LEGO brand, no minifigure trademark, no logos, no watermarks). Chunky plastic bricks with visible studs, 3/4 isometric, sharp edges, no photoreal, no blur. Transparent PNG, one subject per file, ~10% margin, no ground plane, no baked drop shadow, no text. Palette: navy #102870, sky #78c8f8, brick-yellow #fce874, brick-red #e40058, brick-blue #5c94fc, brick-green #40e878, white #fcfcfc, dark stud #181028. Kids 5–6, silhouettes readable at 64px.

Do not draw Mario, Luigi, Batman, Spider-Man, Pikachu, Power Rangers, Ninjago, Voltron, LEGO logo, catalog minifig face, Steve, Alex, Creeper, Enderman, or Minecraft bee 1:1.
